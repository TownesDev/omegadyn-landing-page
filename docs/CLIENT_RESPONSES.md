# 4. Author Bio (Refined Draft)

Anthony Townes is a Christian author, veteran, former actor, public speaker, and lifelong creative whose work blends imagination, discipline, and lived experience. Through Omegadyn Press, he develops stories and projects that explore humanity, sacrifice, technology, faith, and resilience.

Drawing from years of military service, public-facing professions, and a lifelong passion for storytelling, Anthony writes across multiple genres including science fiction, nonfiction, poetry, and speculative fiction. His work often focuses on flawed individuals placed under extraordinary pressure — people forced to confront identity, morality, loss, and purpose.

He is the author of *Interview with AI*, a nonfiction exploration of creativity, responsibility, and artificial intelligence, and is currently developing *Space Cowboy*, a military science fiction saga centered on survival, conflict, and the cost of vengeance.

When not writing, Anthony continues building Omegadyn as a creative and technology-focused brand dedicated to publishing, web development, and future digital ventures.
